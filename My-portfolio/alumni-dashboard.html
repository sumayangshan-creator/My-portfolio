Student alumni
portal dashboard.

01 / Overview

Put the community first.

The dashboard welcomes the user and gathers important updates in one place, including upcoming events and alumni spotlights.

02 / Quick actions

Help users move quickly.

Profile updates, finding alumni, joining discussions, and finding jobs are placed as clear actions around the main content.

03 / Connection

Turn browsing into belonging.

Recommended alumni and mentorship cards create direct opportunities to connect, learn, and support one another.
