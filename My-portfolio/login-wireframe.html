01 Wireframe study

Login and
recovery flow.

01 / Purpose

Make access straightforward.

The first screen gives students a focused login form using their Student ID and password, so the main task is easy to understand.

02 / Recovery

Keep the next step visible.

The “Forgot Password?” link leads to a recovery form where a student can enter their ID, create a new password, and confirm it.

03 / Design note

Simple structure, clear action.

The rounded containers separate the two states and make the intended path from login to recovery easy to follow.
